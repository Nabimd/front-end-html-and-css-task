Black Myth Wukong

Black Myth:Wukong is a single-player action role-playing game (APRG) heavily inspired by the classical chinese novel journey to the west and elements characteristic of the "Soulsike" subgenre. Feng ji (co-founder and CEO of game science,who also served as the game's director and producer).players control the "Destined One,"a surrogate protagonist modeled after Sun Wukong(The Monkey King).
